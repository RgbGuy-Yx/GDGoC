# GDGoC VJIT Recruitment Portal

The official recruitment and candidate evaluation portal for **Google Developer Groups on Campus (GDGoC) VJIT**.

Designed to streamline the annual student recruitment drive, this portal serves as the centralized workspace for organisers and domain leads to manage student applications, conduct interview rounds, review submissions, and evaluate prospective core team and executive members across all domains.

---

### Purpose

Every year, GDGoC welcomes hundreds of enthusiastic students aspiring to learn, build, and lead technology initiatives on campus. The portal facilitates a fair, organized, and transparent selection process:

- **Candidate Management**: Ingests student application responses with accurate domain mapping and candidate tracking.
- **Academic Year Filtering**: Segregates applicants across 1st, 2nd, and 3rd years to conduct interview rounds in designated stages.
- **Domain Pipelines**: Manages recruitment pipelines across all key club tracks:
  - **Technical (Team Terminal)**: Software engineering, web/cloud development, algorithms, and open source.
  - **Design (Team Pixelle)**: UI/UX, product design, branding, visual assets, and illustration.
  - **Event Management (Team EventOps)**: Logistics, event execution, speaker outreach, and operational planning.
  - **Marketing (Team Marketron)**: Social media campaigns, content strategy, storytelling, and community engagement.
  - **Production (Team Chitrakaar)**: Photography, cinematography, video editing, and stage media production.
- **Structured Evaluations**: Domain leads record qualitative feedback, technical scores, and candidate recommendations.
- **Candidate Transfers**: Enables team leads to transfer versatile applicants across domains when a candidate shows strong potential for another team.
- **Organiser Overview**: Provides club leads and organisers with real-time visibility into overall progress, domain statistics, and cross-team shortlisted candidates.

---

### Access & Security

Access to the recruitment portal is restricted to authorized GDGoC VJIT organisers and designated domain team leads using unique security access credentials.
